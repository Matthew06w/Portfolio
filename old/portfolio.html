SHEET 02 / PROJECT

Robotic Sheet Repositioning System

BUILD + RESEARCH PAPER — IN PROGRESS
VIEW 02-A — ISO — IMAGE PLACEHOLDER

A mobile robot built to help caregivers reposition bedridden patients without direct contact — reducing physical strain on staff and the risk of pressure injuries. A curved, padded single-finger hook end-effector engages a reinforced loop sewn into a repositioning sheet, lifting one side to gently tilt or roll the patient.

The robot moves on a wheeled base between beds, positions itself, captures the sheet loop, lifts, holds, and releases — all without touching the patient directly. Alongside the build, I'm writing this up as a research paper.

End-effectorSingle-finger padded hook
InterfaceReinforced sheet loop / pocket
MobilityWheeled base, bed-to-bed

SHEET 03 / PROJECT

Solar-Powered Bicycle Light

BUILD IN PROGRESS

A bicycle light rebuilt around a salvaged LED circuit board, charged entirely by sunlight. I sized a solar panel and battery to the salvaged electronics, then soldered the panel and a TP4056 charging module into the circuit so the light tops itself up during the day.

Everything lives inside a custom case I designed in Onshape, with a recess sized precisely to the 84.5 × 55.5 mm solar panel, and a mount built to clip onto a standard handlebar holder.

PowerSolar panel + Li-ion, via TP4056
Core electronicsSalvaged LED driver PCB
HousingCustom enclosure, modeled in Onshape
MountStandard handlebar clamp
